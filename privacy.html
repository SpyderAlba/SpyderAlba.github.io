Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

What this app accesses

Skill Builder requests the read-only Google scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/yt-analytics.readonly. It is used only by the owner of the Databricks Skill Builder YouTube channel to read that channel's own analytics: per-video views, watch time, and subscribers gained.

How data is used

The analytics are stored in a private internal database and shown on an internal dashboard to track the channel's performance. The app does not access any other Google account, does not read personal information about viewers, and does not upload, edit, or delete any YouTube content.

Sharing

Data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Access is limited to the channel's own contributors.

Retention and revocation

Aggregate channel metrics are kept for as long as they're useful for reporting. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Skill Builder's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
